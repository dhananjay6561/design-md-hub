Do
  • Use dark navy — it makes Render instantly recognizable
  • Use teal on deploy status — it's Render's "all green"
  • Use monospace for logs, env vars, and service URLs
  • Show deploy timestamps in relative time

Don't

  • Don't use grey-dark background — navy is the identity
  • Don't mix purple accent with teal in one component
  • Don't animate deploy log lines during incidents
  • Don't round corners beyond 10px
