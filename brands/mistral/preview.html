Guardrails
DO
Anchor light UI on the cream surfaces (#FBFBF8 → #EBE9E0) — the warm tint is identity, not plain white
Keep the flame mark's six colors fixed and in top-warm→bottom-cool order
Use orange #FF5229 as the single brand action color; blue/green/red carry info/success/danger
Pair Space Grotesk (display) with Space Mono (code, model IDs, metrics) — one role each
Use navy #151524, not black, as the dark canvas
DON'T
Blend the flame squares into a smooth gradient — they are discrete pixels
Use brand orange on large surfaces — it's an accent, not a background
Use pure #000000 / #FFFFFF for text or page — use zinc on cream/navy
Mix font roles (mono for headings, display for code)
Recolor the logo mark to a single hue — its multicolor is the brand
