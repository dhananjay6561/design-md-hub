06 — Guardrails

Do's & Don'ts

Principles that keep Railway warm, purposeful, and trustworthy without becoming cluttered.

✓
Do
  • Use violet/purple accent for all interactive highlights
  • Keep the canvas view clean — services are the content
  • Use status colors consistently: green=live, yellow=building, red=error
  • Animate deployments — motion communicates progress
✕
Don't
  • Use harsh drop shadows — glow effects instead
  • Clutter the canvas with too much metadata per card
  • Use light backgrounds inside the app — dark only
  • Use more than one glow effect per screen
  • Show raw stack traces inline — collapse behind "show details"
