Guardrails
Do
  • Severity colors are life-safety semantics — never repurpose
  • P1 incidents must have prominent red treatment
  • Service names always in monospace
  • Resolved state must feel calming — green, not grey
  • Acknowledge button always visible, never behind hover
Don't
  • Don't use grey for resolved — it reads as deprioritized
  • Don't reuse P-level colors for other meanings
  • Don't truncate incident titles
  • Don't hide on-call person behind a click
  • Don't use color alone for severity — always show P-label
