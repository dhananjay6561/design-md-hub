Guardrails
Do
  • Dark navy on all surfaces — it's MongoDB's identity
  • Use monospace for queries, JSON, and collection names
  • Use bright green sparingly — CTAs and status only
  • Dark text on green buttons — navy contrast demands it
  • Show document counts and query times in monospace
Don't
  • Don't use grey or black background — navy is the signal
  • Don't use green for errors or warnings
  • Don't round corners beyond 10px
  • Don't use thin font weights on navy backgrounds
  • Don't omit connection status from database UIs
