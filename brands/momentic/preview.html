Do
Keep the canvas warm off-white #F5F3F2 with near-black ink and one electric lime for action.
Put black text on the lime button — never white.
Write test steps & CLI in Space Mono; the CTA is an uppercase mono command.
Color test state by meaning: green pass, amber running, red fail/bug.
Don't
Use white text on the lime button, or dull the lime toward yellow-green.
Swap the warm paper for a cold grey — the warmth is part of the identity.
Set test steps or selectors in a proportional sans — they're mono.
Add a second saturated accent — lime is the only one.
