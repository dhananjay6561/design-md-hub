Do
Anchor on a warm cream canvas with the blurred heart gradient (blue→magenta→orange) as atmosphere.
Treat the heart as the logo & soul; the gradient carries AI/generating states and CTAs.
Keep type in Inter (code in JetBrains Mono) and copy plain-spoken & friendly.
Make the prompt → app flow the centerpiece — a calm box, then an app that assembles itself.
Use fully rounded pill buttons and soft floating cards.
Don't
Put the gradient on everything — it's a big soft bloom, not a fill for all buttons and text.
Introduce a competing hue; blue→magenta→orange is the whole palette (magenta is the flat accent).
Make the gradient harsh or banded — it's heavily blurred and dreamy.
Use sharp corners or a cold marketing canvas — Lovable is warm & rounded.
Replace the heart with a generic sparkle / bot icon.
