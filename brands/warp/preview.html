Group commands & output into blocks — hover to raise, click to select.
Keep the canvas near-black with one lavender-purple accent for state (active block, prompt, agent).
Use JetBrains Mono inside blocks; The Future only for display headings.

Don't

Add a second bright accent — lavender carries the brand.
Use heavy shadows between blocks or oversized radii — Warp is tight and technical.
Set body or terminal text in The Future — it's a display face only.
