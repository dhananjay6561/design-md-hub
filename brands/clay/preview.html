Do
Anchor on a warm cream canvas with crisp black UI — color brings the joy.
Bring in the clay palette (blue, orange, pink, green, yellow, red) for illustration & category color.
Set UI & tables in Roobert; use Canela serif only for large editorial lines.
Make the enrichment waterfall legible — providers in order, ✓/✗, credits only on hits.
Keep the rainbow-arch logo and soft, tactile claymation style.
Don't
Reduce Clay to one flat accent — it's cream + black + a playful multi-color clay world.
Set dense tables or body in Canela — the serif is editorial display only.
Use hard, flat drop shadows on the clay art — it's sculpted and soft.
Present enrichment as a single API call — the point is the waterfall.
Make the palette neon or garish — warm, matte, clay-like tones.
