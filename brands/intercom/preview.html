DO
Lead with black and warm cream paper; use blue/orange as accents
Use electric blue #000CE1 for the Messenger, interactive state, links
Keep the Messenger human — Fin branding, quick replies, typing dots
Use Inter for interface text; reserve MediumLL for display
Set IDs and metadata in the mono stack
DON'T
Don't use the pre-rebrand sky blue #1F8EFA — current accent is #000CE1
Don't cool the cream to plain white — the warmth is the identity
Don't invent navy dark surfaces; step within warm near-black
Don't overuse orange — it's a highlight, not a surface
Don't strip Fin's identity from the Messenger
