Do
Anchor on a near-black indigo canvas fading up to a glowing purple horizon.
Use the one indigo→purple→magenta gradient for the mark, CTAs and glows.
Make the network literal — backlinks, a backlinks panel, a node-and-edge graph.
Set titles & wordmark in Aeonik Pro; body in Inter with roomy line-height.
Give accents a soft glow, not a hard shadow.
Don't
Use a flat white canvas as the primary identity — Reflect is nocturnal (light mode is secondary).
Introduce a competing hue — stay in the indigo→magenta purple family.
Show notes as isolated cards with no links — the point is the network.
Set body text in the display face, or crowd the editor.
Make the gradient neon/garish — it's a soft luminous glow.
