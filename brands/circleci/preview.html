DO
Use #00DB74 as the brand green — dull legacy greens are retired.
Keep status colors strictly semantic: green passed / red failed / blue running / bronze on-hold.
Put branch names, SHAs, job names, and durations in monospace.
Pulse running jobs; make failed jobs impossible to miss.
Use black text on the green button (green is too bright for white).
DON'T
Don't use the Morph pastel gradient inside product UI — hero only.
Don't put white text on #00DB74.
Don't repurpose build-status colors for decoration.
Don't hide duration on completed jobs.
Don't set body copy in the pixel face — short uppercase labels only.
