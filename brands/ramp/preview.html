Do
Keep the canvas warm off-white #f4f2ef with warm near-black ink — the warmth is the brand.
Reserve Ramp yellow #e4f222 for primary actions & highlights, always with ink text.
Set everything in TWK Lausanne — light 350 for body, 700 for display.
Use green to celebrate money & time saved — that's Ramp's whole story.
Render money in tabular mono so columns align.
Don't
Don't tint the yellow toward gold or orange, or wash it across a background.
Don't put white text on the yellow, or use yellow for body copy.
Don't swap the warm paper for a cold pure-grey — it flattens the identity.
Don't turn the blue into a primary button — it's for links & data.
Don't add a second display typeface — Lausanne carries it all.
