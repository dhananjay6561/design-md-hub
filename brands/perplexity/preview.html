Answer Block

Quantum entanglement is a phenomenon where two particles become correlated in such a way that the quantum state of one cannot be described independently of the other1, regardless of the distance separating them.

This was famously described by Einstein as "spooky action at a distance"2. When a measurement is made on one particle, it instantaneously affects the other particle's state. The effect can be encoded as |ψ⟩ = (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2.

06 — Guardrails

Do's & Don'ts

Design decisions that keep Perplexity's interface trustworthy, readable, and answer-first.

✓
Do
  • Let the answer be the hero — minimize surrounding UI chrome
  • Use teal accent sparingly — links and one CTA max per view
  • Keep line lengths under 75 characters for readability
  • Show source citations prominently — trust is the product
✕
Don't
  • Add decorative gradients or background textures
  • Use heavy shadows — keep the UI flat and clean
  • Use more than 2 font weights on a single screen
  • Add animations that delay showing the answer
  • Use colored backgrounds for content sections
