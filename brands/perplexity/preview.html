How does vector search work in practice?
Vector search converts data into high-dimensional embeddings 1 and finds semantically similar items by measuring distance — typically cosine similarity — between those vectors 2. Unlike keyword search, it captures meaning rather than exact string matches, making it ideal for AI-powered retrieval and recommendation systems 3.
