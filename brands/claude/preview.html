05 — Tone & Guardrails

Rules that protect the aesthetic

A design system is only as good as the decisions it prevents. These are non-negotiable principles.

✓
Do
  • Use generous whitespace — crowded UI feels anxious
  • Prefer warm neutrals over pure white or pure black
  • Use the coral accent sparingly — one CTA per view
  • Animate with ease curves, keep transitions under 200ms
✕
Don't
  • Use harsh drop shadows or heavy borders
  • Use blue as a primary color — it conflicts with the brand
  • Use all-caps headings anywhere in the interface
  • Use more than 2 font weights on a single screen
  • Add decorative gradients or glass-morphism effects
