DO
Use Twilio Red #F22F46 as the one loud primary — CTAs, the bug, emphasis
Keep the bug (four dots in a ring) monochrome red on paper, white, or ink
Use warm paper #FDF7F4 for page backgrounds — it's part of the identity
Use blue #0263E0 for links; reserve red for brand and destructive
Pair every message status with its colored chip and a real SM… SID
DON'T
Use random reds — the brand red is #F22F46
Recolor or split the bug's fill — it's a single-color mark
Use pure black #000 — Twilio's ink is #000D25
Cool the background to plain white everywhere — keep the warm paper tint
Fake phone numbers or SIDs — use +E.164 and SM + hex
